Off the shelf.

Cart to Shelf is removed. Everything it stored — the ISBN cache and the list of books it had already sent — was local to your browser and went with it. There's no account to close and no server holding anything, because there never was one.

Your Goodreads shelf and your Amazon cart are untouched.

If it broke, that's worth knowing

The two things most likely to have gone wrong, and what they mean:

Books wouldn't add
Goodreads changed something undocumented. The CSV path is built for exactly this and doesn't depend on their markup.
The scan found nothing
Amazon reshuffled its cart layout. Usually a one-line fix once someone reports it.
